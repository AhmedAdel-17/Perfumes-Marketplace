Name Candidates — D-1

الاسم

ثمانية مرشّحين، كلٌّ منها معروض كعلامة نصّية حقيقية لا كقائمة. الاسم يُحكم عليه بالعين لا بالوصف — كيف يبدو مُتباعد الحروف، وكيف يقف بجانب العربية.

Selection criteria
Meaning
Rooted in fragrance, trace or craft — never a generic category word.
Distinctiveness
Descriptive words make weak trademarks. "Perfume" cannot be owned.
Bilingual form
Must hold as letterspaced Bodoni caps and as Amiri Arabic.
Not a first name
The positioning is "more credible than an Instagram seller." A personal name undercuts exactly that.
01
ATHAR
أثر
ˈA-thar
The trace left behind — an impression, a mark. In the plural, آثار: Egypt's antiquities.
FORA perfect double meaning. A scent is literally a trace left behind, and آثار is the most Egyptian word there is. Concept and country in one word.
FORFive clean caps. Letterspaces beautifully — the two A's give it symmetry.
FORAlready the working mark in your design references, so the visual language was built around it.
RISKThe most likely of all eight to be taken. "Athar" is used across the Gulf in fragrance and lifestyle.
RISKThe antiquities association can read museum rather than modern if the visual language ever softened.
DOMAssume the exact .com is gone. Screen athar.eg, atharparfums, dar-athar.
02
DIYAR
ديار
di-ˈYAR
The houses — plural of دار. The word your own interface already uses: ٤٢ دار عطور.
FORStructurally the most accurate name on this list. You are not a perfume brand — you are a collection of perfume houses. This name says exactly that.
FORIt flatters sellers. "Brand independence" is a founding principle, and a name meaning "the houses" makes them the subject rather than the inventory.
RISKHeavily used in Gulf real estate (Diyar Al Muharraq and others). Different class, but it dilutes distinctiveness and search results.
RISKSays nothing about scent on its own. Needs a descriptor lockup — DIYAR · PARFUMS.
DOMScreen diyar.eg, diyarparfums, diyar.co.
03
KHITAM
ختام
khi-ˈTAM
The seal, the closing. From خِتامُهُ مِسْك — "its seal is musk."
FORThe deepest cultural resonance of any candidate. Educated Egyptians complete the phrase instantly, and it already links the word to musk.
FORThe most distinctive, therefore the strongest trademark. Nobody in fragrance is using it.
FOR"Seal" carries a second meaning you actually need: verification. Your platform seals what it approves.
RISKSix letters with a KH digraph. Harder for a non-Arabic speaker, and the Latin lockup is wider than the others.
DOMLikely the most available. Screen khitam.com, khitam.eg.
04
AREEJ
أريج
a-ˈREEJ
The diffusion of sweet scent — the classical Arabic word for fragrance spreading through air.
FORArguably the most beautiful pure-fragrance word in Arabic. It describes projection — a field you literally have in the catalogue.
RISKIt is a common Egyptian female first name. That reads as a personal Instagram shop — the exact impression the platform exists to escape.
RISKDescriptive of the category, which weakens trademark protection.
DOMAlmost certainly taken across the region.
05
MAQAM
مقام
ma-ˈQAM
A mode, a station, a standing — the melodic system of Arabic music.
FORThe best metaphor available. A perfume is a composition in three movements — top, heart, base. Your notes pyramid is already built like a score.
FORVisually superb letterspaced. Symmetrical, opens and closes on M.
RISKThe musical association is strong enough that people may expect audio, not scent.
RISKمقام also means a shrine or saint's tomb. Worth checking that reading lands cleanly in Egypt.
DOMScreen maqam.eg, maqamparfums.
06
NAFAS
نَفَس
ˈNA-fas
A single breath — one inhalation.
FORThe most modern and minimal of the eight. Scent is only ever experienced through breath.
FORFive letters, easy in any language, no digraphs.
RISKOne diacritic away from نَفْس — self, soul. Spoken aloud the two blur, and a name that must be explained is a weak name.
RISKReads slightly clinical — closer to wellness than to a fragrance house.
DOMScreen nafas.eg. Used by wellness apps regionally.
07
SHADHA
شذا
ˈSHA-dha
Diffused sweetness — the lingering trail of a scent in a room.
FORThe most poetic word here. شذا in Amiri is genuinely beautiful — three letters, one sweeping curve.
RISKThe transliteration defeats it. SHADHA is six letters with two digraphs and no obvious pronunciation. The Arabic is elegant; the Latin is not.
RISKAlso used as a female name.
DOMTransliteration ambiguity fragments the domain — shadha / shatha / shaza.
08
AWAN
أوان
a-ˈWAN
The right time, the proper season — and near-homophone of آنية, vessels.
FORShortest and cleanest Latin form on the list. Four letters, perfect for an app icon.
FOR"Season" connects to a real catalogue field — season and occasion are how people actually choose a scent.
RISKThe most abstract. It needs a descriptor to mean anything at all.
RISKThe vessel reading is a near-miss, not a real one — a nice story in a deck, invisible to a customer.
DOMShort domains are expensive. Expect awan.com to be held.

Device-only wins at icon size. At 60×60 on a home screen the wordmark is illegible in every candidate — which is an argument for the name-independent device, not against any name. The wordmark belongs on the splash and the storefront, not the icon.

Recommendation

Screen KHITAM, DIYAR and ATHAR — in that order.

KHITAM first because distinctiveness is the one criterion you cannot fix later. It is the strongest trademark, nobody in fragrance holds it, and "the seal" carries the verification meaning your platform is actually built on. Its weakness — the KH — is a typography problem, and typography is solvable.

DIYAR second because it describes the business more accurately than anything else here, and it puts your sellers in the name.

ATHAR third — not because it is weaker as a word, but because it is the most likely to be unavailable. Screen it early so you find out fast.

I have not verified any domain or trademark. I have no way to check registries from here, so every availability note above is a probability, not a fact. Screening is a real task with a real cost — an Egyptian IP agent should run all three through the Trademark Office in the relevant classes (3 for cosmetics and fragrance, 35 for retail and marketplace services) before you commit. Register the domain the same week the search clears.
