# Ownership model

Doc 06 §1 assigns accountable and supporting owners; doc 05 §3 divides founder
accountability. At pilot the founders wear every hat; the table records the **role**, who
holds it now, and when it becomes a hire (see `04-staffing-evolution.md`).

| Area | Accountable (doc 06 §1 / doc 05 §3) | Supporting | Holder at pilot | Decision rights (doc 05 §7) | First hire trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seller acquisition and commercial terms | business founder | ops, legal | business founder | non-standard terms: both founders | > 20 houses in pipeline |
| Seller success (onboarding, training, retention) | marketplace operations | category | business founder | — | > 10 live houses |
| Catalogue and category (vocabularies, notes dictionary, merchandising) | marketplace operations / category manager | legal | business founder (+ technical founder for vocabulary migrations) | — | > 300 SKUs |
| Moderation (listings, claims, reviews) | marketplace operations | legal | business founder | suspension: both | > 50 listings/week |
| Customer support | operations lead | seller success, technical | both founders (rota) | P0: immediate escalation to both | > 15 tickets/day |
| Order operations and exceptions | operations lead | seller success, technical | business founder | — | > 30 orders/day |
| Finance: reconciliation, payouts, statements | finance owner | ops, technical | business founder (accountant external) | payouts: second approver = technical founder at pilot | > 10 houses or first PSP |
| Marketing and merchandising | business/marketing | category, analytics | business founder | budgets: both | budget approved (D-9) |
| Analytics definitions | business founder (FACT, `ANALYTICS_DEFINITIONS.md`) | technical (computation) | as stated | — | — |
| Legal / compliance | named compliance owner + counsel | all | business founder + external counsel | policy changes: both | PDPL enforcement (~Oct 2026) |
| Privacy and security | named owners (doc 06 §1) | all | privacy: business founder; security: technical founder | security incident: technical founder contains, informs | — |
| Product | product/technology founder (design execution, scope with both) | business founder (market policy, acceptance) | technical founder | scope: both | — |
| Technology, delivery, infrastructure | technical founder | — | technical founder | security-critical architecture: both | > 1 engineer needed |

## Rules

- Primary accountability is not unilateral authority (doc 05 §3).
- Neither founder changes economics, customer policy or security-critical architecture
  alone (doc 04 §9).
- Two-person visibility on bank/payment access and periodic reconciliation (doc 05 §9).
- Every alert has an owner (`docs/architecture/09`).
