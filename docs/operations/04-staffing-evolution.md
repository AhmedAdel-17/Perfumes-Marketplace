# Staffing evolution

By growth stage (`docs/scaling/02`), with the trigger that justifies each role. Numbers are
RECOMMENDATIONS shaped by doc 06's ownership map, not budgets (D-9 open). External
partners (counsel, accountant, trademark agent, photographer) are contracted, not hired.

| Stage | Houses | Orders/month (order of magnitude) | Team | Triggers for the next role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | 3–5 | tens | two founders (all hats) + external counsel/accountant + contracted photographer | — |
| Stage 0 late | 5–20 | hundreds | + operations lead (support, exceptions, seller success) | > 15 tickets/day or > 30 orders/day; founders' onboarding time > 50 % |
| Stage 1 early | 20–100 | thousands | + seller success/onboarding specialist; + part-time finance/reconciliation; + category/content editor; + second engineer | > 10 onboardings/month; payouts > 20 houses/cycle; > 300 SKUs; roadmap backlog beyond one engineer |
| Stage 1 late | 100–300 | tens of thousands | + support team (2–3), + finance owner full time, + marketing/growth, + QA/ops engineer | support SLA misses; PSP + courier reconciliation daily; paid campaigns running |
| Stage 2 | 300–1,000+ | — | functional leads; ops in shifts; data analyst; compliance officer (PDPL DPO if required — counsel) | see `docs/scaling/04` |

## Hiring principles

Hire for the stream that is failing its cadence, not for the org chart · operations before
marketing · every hire inherits a written cadence and a screen · no role owns a number
without a definition · founders keep customer support contact until Stage 1 (the fastest
way to learn) · engineering stays one person until P0/P1 are done and the roadmap is
evidence-driven.

## What not to hire early

A CTO/VP-level engineering hire (the technical founder is that), a brand agency (the
doctrine exists; a photographer is the need), a BI analyst (definitions exist; `/ops/insights`
suffices), a warehouse team (Phase 3 gate), an app team (Phase 4 gate).
