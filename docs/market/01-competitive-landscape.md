# Competitive landscape

**Method and limits.** A structured web scan performed on **18 September 2026** using the
doc 02 §5 framework. Each entry records what was *observed* at a cited URL and date;
snippets not opened are marked **(search snippet, not page-verified)**. Nothing here is a
market fact beyond what the source states; traction figures are the sources' own. This
scan does **not** satisfy doc 02 §5's requirement for one evidence row per competitor with
screenshots — that remains a validation-sprint task. **No claim of "first in Egypt" is made
or supported** (doc 02 §1).

## 2. Players observed, by doc 02 §4 segment

  fragrance pages, search snippet, 18 Sep 2026).

  vendorhub, 18 Sep 2026).

## 3. Patterns

1. Multi-brand perfume e-commerce in Egypt positions on **"original / authentic imported"
   and discounts**; nobody observed positions on *verified local makers with structured
   scent data*.
2. **COD dominates and fails often** (cited 20–35 % first attempt) — an operations
   problem every player shares and few solve publicly.
3. Local houses are **discovered through press lists and social**, and sell through
   Instagram; a structured, comparable catalogue of them was not observed.
4. Generic marketplaces charge **published category commissions with minimums** (Jumia 13 %
   incl. VAT, EGP 10 min) — a reference point for seller conversations.
5. Local growth is in **affordable mists/splashes** (Euromonitor summary) — the B-3
   boundary question has commercial weight.
