**Perfume Egypt is the nearest specialist competitor found, and it is aimed at a different

still means "the first specialist marketplace for Egyptian perfume" **cannot be said**, and
that it may be the first place a house compares DIYAR's terms with.

**What the house channels show.** Returns promises range from none to 14 days. Two
published policies contradict themselves or are unfinished templates, one site fails its TLS

recourse today depends on which house's policy page they happened to read. It is rank 4
evidence (observed storefronts). It is not proof buyers care — P0.4.3 asks them.
