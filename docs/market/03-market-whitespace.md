# Market whitespace

Derived only from the patterns in `01` and `02`. Each whitespace names the unmet job (doc
02 §5: "specific unmet job — never 'we will be better'"), the evidence, and the risk that
the whitespace is empty for a reason.

| # | Whitespace | Unmet job | Evidence | Why it might be empty for a reason (RISK) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | **Structured, comparable data on Egyptian local perfumes** | "compare local perfumes on the same terms before I buy" (CJ1) | no player observed offers notes/concentration/performance-with-source across local houses; press lists are prose | local houses may not be able to supply structured data (verify in interviews) |
| W2 | **Verified-maker trust** (who made it, what is in it, return route) | "buy from a page someone checked" | incumbents' trust word is "original imported"; local houses are trusted page-by-page | verification cost per house; houses may refuse documentation |
| W3 | **Reviews gated by delivery** | "read reviews from people who actually bought" | not observed as a stated mechanism in the scan | review volume takes time; forgery pressure |
| W4 | **Seller operating system for social-first houses** | "stop running orders through DMs" (SJ1–SJ4) | houses sell via Instagram per press; DM operations are the documented pain | houses may prefer WhatsApp habits; SaaS willingness unproven |
| W5 | **Fair asymmetric fee** | "don't tax my own followers" (SJ5) | generic marketplaces publish flat category commissions with minimums | attribution disputes; complexity for sellers |
| W6 | **COD operations done well** (verification by risk, reason codes, allocation) | "not lose stock and cash to refusals" | 20–35 % first-attempt failure nationally (cited) | courier behaviour outside our control |
| W7 | **Arabic-first, quiet, category-authority brand** | "a place that takes local perfume seriously" | incumbents shout discounts; local pride content exists in press | brand alone does not convert; needs W1–W3 |
| W8 | **Body mists/splashes as an entry category** | affordable local mass growth (Euromonitor summary) | B-3 open | dilutes "perfume house" positioning |
| W9 | **Sampling of local perfumes** | "try before I buy a full bottle" | decant sellers exist for imported/Arabian originals; none observed for local houses | Phase 4 by doc 04; operations-heavy |

## Priority (RECOMMENDATION)

W1 + W2 + W3 are one product (the shopfront's trust and data layer); W4 + W5 are one product
(the seller OS with attribution); W6 is operations policy. These five are the MVP. W7 is
the brand that carries them. W8 is a boundary decision; W9 is deferred by the pack.
