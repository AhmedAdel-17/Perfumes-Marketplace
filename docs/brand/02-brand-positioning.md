# Brand positioning

## 1. Positioning statement (DOCUMENTED doc 01 §5)

For Egyptian perfume buyers overwhelmed by fragmented options, [DIYAR] is the specialist
marketplace that makes local fragrance easy to compare and safer to buy. Unlike generic
marketplaces or isolated brand pages, it combines standardised scent information, verified
reviews and category-specific seller standards.

## 2. Positioning for sellers (RECOMMENDATION, derived from doc 01 §3 and doc 03)

For serious Egyptian perfume houses selling through social pages and shops, [DIYAR] is
the operating system and marketplace that gives them a storefront, orders, payouts and
new customers — charging a low fee on the customers they bring and a marketplace rate
only on the ones it brings.

## 3. Category frame

We are **not** a beauty marketplace, a perfume shop, or a Gulf-fragrance importer. We are
the place where **Egyptian local fragrance** is organised: houses, their perfumes, the
facts about them, and the people who bought them. Category boundaries are D-6 (open);
"body mists and splashes" — the fastest-growing mass segment per Euromonitor's Egypt
summary (FACT, cited in `docs/market/01`) — is a boundary decision (B-3) with brand
consequences: including them widens the value wedge; excluding them keeps "perfume" pure.

## 4. Proof points (each must remain true)

| Claim | Proof |
|---|---|
| Every house is checked | 8-control verification, visible per house |
| Every perfume is described the same way | schema, vocabularies, bands with source |
| Every review comes from a delivery | gate in three layers |
| No hidden costs | per-shipment fee before payment |
| One relationship | one phone, one tracking page, one support route |
| Fair to makers | attribution shown; low rate on own customers |

## 5. Positioning traps (RISK)

"First in Egypt" — doc 02 §1 forbids it until a dated competitor search is complete; the
`docs/market/` scan found adjacent players (imported-perfume e-tailers, decant sellers,
generic marketplaces) and **no verified specialist local-perfume marketplace**, which is
still not proof of absence. "100 % original/authentic" — the language of the importers;
our claim is *verified maker*, not *authenticated designer goods*.
