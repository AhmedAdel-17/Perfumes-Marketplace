# Visual identity direction

Strategy, not implementation. The implemented doctrine (`docs/DESIGN_DOCTRINE.md`), tokens
and design references are FACT and remain the concrete expression of the storefront
language; this document places them in a brand system with two related languages.

## 1. Visual territory

**A lit room, not a white shelf.** The storefront is a dark, warm, photographic space in
which objects are lit and named. The tools are a bright, exact workroom. Both are made of
the same materials — the same three colours in two directions (noir/bone/brass and
paper/graphite/bronze, FACT), the same faces, the same hairlines — used at different
densities.

## 2. Colour philosophy

One accent, used for moments (doctrine law IV). Colour never carries meaning alone. House
colours enter only through photographs and house marks, never through the chrome: the
frame stays neutral so a hundred houses can sit in it. Data colours (note tiers, families)
exist for visualisation only.

Decision to confirm (OPEN, FD-1): the noir default. The 7 Sep review recommended keeping it;
paper is the visitor's option.

## 3. Typography philosophy

Arabic sets the scale (Amiri for headings, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for everything else); Latin
is a label (Bodoni Moda, small caps, letterspaced). Two ramps: **Maison** (display from 52
px at desktop) and **Atelier** (headings capped at 26 px). Numbers are tabular and carry a
letterspaced currency label.

## 4. Photography direction — see `07-photography-guidelines.md`

Photography is the identity's largest lever and its current absence its largest defect
(FACT: identical illustrated flacons). The illustrated flacon remains the *placeholder*,
labelled as such in the seller portal.

## 5. Iconography

One bespoke set (40 icons, 1.3 stroke, FACT). Directional icons flip in RTL; logos and
objects do not. No third-party icon library.

## 6. Illustration

Minimal: the flacon placeholder, note-family glyphs, and diagrammatic elements (pyramid,
evolution curve). No decorative illustration, no mascots, no stock.

## 7. Editorial direction

Long-form house stories, "how to read a perfume" education, category features, all in the
CMS block set (FACT). Editorial images follow the photography rules. Editorial is how the
platform expresses category authority without shouting.

## 9. Seller brand presentation rules

| Element | House controls | Platform controls |
|---|---|---|
| Mark (logo) | supplies; monochrome and colour versions | placement, size, clear space; fallback monogram |
| Banner | supplies a 3:1 image following the photo rules | crop, overlay for legibility |
| Story | writes | moderates claims; edits for clarity with consent |
| Product photographs | supplies | verifies dimensions/ownership; rejects designer imitations |
| Colours | appear in photographs and mark only | chrome remains the frame |
| Price | sets | displays in the standard format |
| Voice | its own inside its storefront and story | the frame's voice everywhere else |

Houses may use the platform's verified mark on their own channels under a short rule set
(RECOMMENDATION; OPEN for founder approval).

## 10. Marketplace trust elements (visual)

Verified mark (one glyph + word, outlined, never a gold badge), rating summary (number,
count, "verified purchases"), source line under bands, delivery line with fee, return
eligibility line, sponsored label. All quiet, all textual or hairline-bound.

## 11. Customer-facing visual hierarchy

1. The object (photograph) · 2. the house (mark, name) · 3. the name of the perfume ·
4. the price with its label · 5. trust lines (verified, rating, delivery) · 6. the action.
Everything else recedes.

## 12. Operational interface visual hierarchy

1. What needs doing now (deadline, count) · 2. the identifiers (reference, house, customer)
· 3. the decision controls · 4. detail on expand. Tables over cards for anything with more
than five rows. Status is a word in an outlined pill.

## 13. Two languages, one family

| | Maison (customer) | Atelier (seller and ops) |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | noir default, paper optional | paper default, noir optional |
| Type scale | display-led | body-led, 26 px max heading |
| Density | air | rows |
| Motion | 400–700 ms settles | 120–240 ms, minimal |
| Components | photo, tile, house card, compare, timeline | table, stat, queue row, decision bar, ledger |
| Shared | palette roles, faces, hairlines, radius 0–3, icons, status pills, plural rules, RTL rules | |

## 14. What this direction refuses

Gradients (including the current primary button, FD-7), badges, shadows as separators,
stock photography, Gulf-luxury clichés, a second accent, emoji, mascots, a template look
where every house is the same bottle.
