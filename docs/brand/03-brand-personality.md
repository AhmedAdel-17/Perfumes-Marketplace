# Brand personality

## 1. Personality in five traits

| Trait | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Discerning** | chooses, checks, says why | snobbish, exclusive |
| **Straight** | states prices, sources, policies, states plainly | blunt, cold |
| **Warm** | knows the houses by name; speaks to the buyer as a person | chatty, emoji, exclamation marks (doctrine law VII) |
| **Quiet** | restraint in visuals and copy; no urgency | passive, vague |
| **Egyptian, contemporary** | Cairo now; Arabic first; local craft as normal, not exotic | folkloric, Gulf pastiche, nationalist |

## 2. If the brand were a person (a framing device, not a persona to design for)

A knowledgeable friend who owns a good shop: happy to explain the difference between an
attar and an eau de parfum, tells you when a bottle will actually arrive, tells you which
house made it and what they are like, and would rather lose a sale than sell you the wrong
thing.

## 3. Personality per surface

| Surface | Register |
|---|---|
| Shopfront | editorial, unhurried, precise |
| House storefront | the house's own voice inside our frame; the frame stays quiet |
| Messages (SMS/WhatsApp) | short, factual, courteous; every message answers "what now" |
| Seller portal | operational, respectful, exact (numbers, deadlines, reasons) |
| Ops console | terse, unambiguous, never cute |
| Policy pages | plain Arabic; legal precision after counsel; no jargon |

## 4. Personality guardrails

- Never a superlative without a number.
- Never blame a house to a customer; never blame a customer to a house.
- Never an exclamation mark, emoji, countdown, or "only N left".
- Humour: almost never; a light touch in empty states at most.
- Arabic that sounds authored, not translated (six-form plurals, correct leading — FACT
  practices in the codebase).
