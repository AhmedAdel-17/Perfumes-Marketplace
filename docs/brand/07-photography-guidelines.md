# Photography guidelines

Photography is the product (doctrine §2 "Imagery"). These guidelines are written for
houses, the platform's own shoots, and the moderation checklist. They are the basis for the
listing requirement proposed in `docs/frontend/04-redesign-plan.md` (FD-2, OPEN).

## 1. Requirements per listing (RECOMMENDATION)

| Shot | Purpose | Ratio | Minimum | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary — bottle on dark** | tile, product hero, compare | 4:5 | 1000 × 1250 | bottle centred, full height, on a dark neutral surface, one warm key light from above-behind, real reflection or contact shadow, no props, no text overlay |
| **Secondary — bottle in context** | product gallery, storefront | 4:5 | 1000 × 1250 | in hand, on a surface, with packaging; natural or warm light; nothing that implies a designer brand |
| **Packaging** | policy (sealed/opened), returns evidence | 4:5 | 1000 × 1250 | box and seal visible |
| **House banner** | storefront | 3:1 | 1800 × 600 | atelier, maker, materials, or a still life; no text |
| **House mark** | everywhere | square | 512 × 512 SVG or PNG | monochrome version required |

Ownership confirmation is required for every image (FACT, publish gate). Alt text is
required (FACT). Never upscale; the platform verifies dimensions of what arrived (FACT).

## 2. Light and colour

Warm key, cool fill at most; blacks with detail; liquid visible through glass; no colour
casts that misrepresent the juice. Paper theme still shows the dark plate (doctrine §7).

## 3. What is refused at moderation (doc 06 §4, FD-2)

- Designer bottles, logos or packaging in frame ("inspired by" products photograph *their
  own* bottle only).
- Text overlays, price stickers, "SALE", watermarks.
- Stock or AI-generated product images presented as the product.
- Screenshots, low-resolution phone crops, heavy filters.
- Images the house does not own.

## 4. The platform shoot programme (RECOMMENDATION, OPEN for budget)

For pilot houses: a half-day shoot per house against a standard set (dark surface, one
light), producing primary + secondary + packaging per SKU and a banner. Cost per house is a
budget line in the 90-day plan; benefit: consistent quality across the catalogue at launch
and a concrete, non-rate onboarding incentive (`docs/seller-experience/05`).

## 5. Editorial photography

Same light and colour rules; people allowed; hands and materials preferred over faces;
never stock; credited internally.

## 6. Placeholder policy

Until a verified primary photograph exists, the listing cannot publish (FACT). The
illustrated flacon appears only in the seller portal preview, labelled "لا توجد صورة بعد".
