# Brand principles

Seven principles, each with the rule it produces and the founding-pack source. They govern
copy, design, merchandising, partnerships and how houses are presented.

| # | Principle | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The frame serves the picture** | The platform's visual grammar (type, space, rhythm) is constant; inside a storefront the house's mark, photographs and story lead. The platform never places its mark on a product image. | doc 01 §6 brand independence |
| 2 | **Verified means checked, and says what was checked** | The verified mark always carries its meaning on hover/tap; it is never shown for a house missing a control. | doc 06 §3 |
| 3 | **Numbers carry their source** | Performance bands, ratings, delivery windows and fees state where they come from. | doc 04 §4; CLAUDE.md §4 |
| 4 | **Sponsorship is labelled, ranking is not for sale** | Any paid visibility carries «مُروَّج»; organic order is by quality signals only. | doc 03 §2, doc 06 §10 |
| 5 | **Quiet luxury, loud honesty** | Restraint in visuals; explicitness in money, policy and status. | doctrine; doc 01 §6 transparent economics |
| 6 | **Egyptian, contemporary, plural** | Many houses, many styles; the platform does not impose one aesthetic on the products, only on the frame. Arabic first. | doc 01 §4; D-3 |
| 7 | **One relationship, one voice to the customer** | Messages come from the platform and name the house; the customer never hears two contradicting voices. | doc 01 §6 |

## Application checklist (for any new surface, campaign or partnership)

- [ ] Does the house's identity lead inside its own space, and the frame elsewhere?
- [ ] Is every "verified", "rated", "arrives in" backed by data the screen can show?
- [ ] Is any paid placement labelled?
- [ ] Is there any urgency, superlative or discount shouting? Remove it.
- [ ] Does the Arabic sound authored? Are plurals correct?
- [ ] Would a house be proud to link to this from its bio?
- [ ] Would a first-time buyer know who they are paying and who answers if it goes wrong?
