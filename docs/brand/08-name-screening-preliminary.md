## 3. A risk the register does not list yet: ديار and ديور

An Arabic search for «ديار عطور» ("Diyar perfumes") returned **only Dior** (ديور) on
24 Sep 2026. The two words differ by one letter, ا against و. Two consequences:

1. **Discoverability.** Arabic searches for the name may be corrected to Dior, the first
   brand the doctrine measures itself against. That is an SEO and word-of-mouth cost, in
   the language of the product's first customers.
2. **Similarity.** Whether a one-letter distance from a famous designer mark in Arabic script
   is a problem is exactly the question to put to the IP agent. **It is not a conclusion
   here.** CLAUDE.md §4 already treats designer names as legally sensitive.
