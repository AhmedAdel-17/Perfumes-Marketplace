# Long-term vision

Doc 01 §3: Egypt's most trusted destination and commercial infrastructure for local
fragrance. What that looks like in stages, and what it deliberately never becomes.

## Twelve months (doc 01 §10 definition of progress)

A verified beachhead of active houses, not signed listings; completed orders with positive
contribution and controlled cancellation/refusal; evidence that buyers return directly and
buy across houses; a repeatable onboarding and catalogue-quality process; a documented
legal, tax, data and cosmetics compliance position reviewed in Egypt; clear evidence for or
against an app, central fulfilment and sampling.

## Three years (RECOMMENDATION, contingent on Phase 2 evidence)

The reference catalogue of Egyptian fragrance — the place a house launches a perfume and a
buyer learns what it is. Houses run their operations on the platform whether or not the
marketplace sent the order. Card payment and platform-booked couriers reduce refusals below
the market's. Reviews and indicators make reliability visible and reward it. Category
demand comes from content, creators and search as much as from house audiences.
Seller-side tools are a second revenue line. Selected inventory only where density proved
it.

## Five years and beyond (IDEA, not planned)

Sampling and discovery formats if evidence supports them (doc 04 Phase 4); adjacent local
craft categories only with a documented case; another market only with a documented case
(doc 04 §10 defers international). The asset that travels is the method: verified makers,
structured product truth, transaction-gated reviews, fair attribution.

## What it never becomes

A discount marketplace; a private-label competitor to its houses; a platform that sells
ranking; a data business that surveils buyers; an English-first product for a Gulf
aesthetic; a company that automates the decisions that require a person (approval,
termination, refunds above threshold, review removal).

## The measure of success at every horizon

Completed orders that both sides would repeat: houses that stay because the statement is
right and the demand is real; buyers who come back because the bottle was as described.
