# Strategic thesis

Doc 01's executive thesis: **build the trusted commerce and operating layer for Egyptian
local perfume brands — a focused marketplace for buyers and a practical online-sales
system for sellers.** This document states what must be true for that thesis to hold, and
how each part is tested.

## The thesis in five claims

| # | Claim | Type | Test (evidence rank, doc 02 §3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Egyptian buyers will change behaviour for comparison and verified trust in local perfume | HYPOTHESIS | 30 buyer interviews; cross-house browsing and purchase in the pilot (rank 1) |
| 2 | Serious social-first houses will route real orders through a third-party storefront if the economics respect their own demand | HYPOTHESIS | ≥ 3 houses keep the bio link and route live orders (rank 2) |
| 3 | The asymmetric fee can be attributed fairly and accepted | HYPOTHESIS | attribution live; statements accepted; effective take rate per house without dispute |
| 4 | A completed order can carry positive contribution without hidden subsidy | HYPOTHESIS | inputs recorded (A-2); worksheet filled with rank-1/4 evidence |
| 5 | The category's structured data and reviews become an asset nobody else builds | HYPOTHESIS | catalogue depth per family; reviews per delivered order; incumbents' behaviour |

## What the thesis is not

Not "Amazon for perfumes" (doc 01 §5). Not a logistics company (Phase 3 at most). Not a
brand of perfume. Not a tool for anonymous mixers. Not a discount channel.

## The two-sided asymmetry, stated once

The platform earns more when it creates demand or performs operations; it must not tax
seller-owned demand as if it created it (doc 03 core model). This makes attribution the
single most important product mechanism and the ledger the single most important record.
Today both are unfinished (FACT); the thesis cannot be tested until they work.

## Kill criteria (doc 02 §11 "pause")

Polite interest but no seller commitment, no completed orders, or no contribution path
after the validation sprint and pilot. Recorded in advance so the decision is not
improvised.
