## 3. Outreach and pitch (RECOMMENDATION)

Lead with operations and fairness, not traffic (doc 02 §6 warns against polite yeses):

1. "Show me last week's orders" — listen first (doc 02 §6 script).
2. "Here is what changes: one link, orders in a queue with a clock, messages to your
   customers, a statement you can check."
3. "Here is what it costs: X on your own customers, Y only when we bring the customer, and
   you will see which is which on every order."
4. "Here is what we need: documents, a test order, an SLA, your link in your bio."
5. "Here is what we will not do: sell your customers' data, rank by payment, undercut you."

## 4. Objections and answers

| Objection | Answer (must be true in the product) |
|---|---|
| "You'll take a cut of my own followers." | Low fee on your own customers, proven per order (needs ADR-07 live — do not claim before). |
| "I'll lose my customer relationship." | You get every order's contact to ship; the platform answers support; your storefront is your page. |
| "My photos aren't good enough." | We shoot them (pilot offer). |
| "Paperwork." | Eight controls, one afternoon; we walk you through; it is why buyers trust you. |
| "What if a customer refuses at the door?" | Reason codes; cost allocation policy; phone verification reduces it (state policy honestly; OPEN). |
| "Marketplaces charge 13–25 %." (FACT: Jumia lists health & beauty at 13 % incl. VAT with a 10 EGP minimum, vendor hub accessed 18 Sep 2026) | Our marketplace rate applies only to orders we bring; yours stay at the low fee. |
| "Why not just my own website?" | You keep it; DIYAR adds category discovery, reviews tied to deliveries, and operations you don't build. |

## 5. Onboarding funnel targets (pilot; ASSUMPTIONS)

50 mapped → 15 interviewed → 8 qualified → 5 applied → 3–5 live within weeks 1–4 (doc 02
§9). Track days per stage and the stall reasons (`docs/analytics/04`).

## 6. Seller referral mechanics

Benefit in kind only (no rate changes outside D-4 governance). Recorded as supply CAC.

## 7. Do not

Promise marketplace demand before it is measured · sign non-standard rate terms without
both founders (doc 05 §7) · onboard S4 before verification is possible · exceed five houses
before the payout ledger is live (C1).
