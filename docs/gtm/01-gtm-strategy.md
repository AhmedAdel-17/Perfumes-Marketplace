# Go-to-market strategy

Constraints: no marketing budget is approved (D-9 open); two founders; Cairo/Giza (D-7);
Arabic; COD-first market; the founding pack's rule that a registered user is not an
acquired customer and that seller-routed real orders are the only proof (doc 02).

## 1. Sequence (RECOMMENDATION)

**Supply first, demand through supply, then category demand.**

1. **Supply**: 3–5 verified houses with real weekly demand (S1), one credibility house (S3),
   ideally one with a website (S2-lite). Founder-led, personal, evidence-gated
   (`02-seller-acquisition.md`).
2. **Demand through supply**: each house's existing audience arrives via its DIYAR link in
   the bio; attribution gives the house the low rate, so it has every reason to route
   (`docs/economics/01`). This is the cold-start engine (`04-cold-start-strategy.md`).
3. **Category demand**: once the catalogue spans ≥3 houses per family and reviews exist,
   invest in discovery: content, SEO on house and perfume pages, creators with codes,
   then paid campaigns with attribution (`03-customer-acquisition.md`). Not before, or the
   platform pays CAC to send buyers to a thin shelf.

## 2. Pilot offer to houses (RECOMMENDATION; commercial terms D-4 open)

- No listing fee (doc 03 §8).
- Low fee on their own customers; marketplace rate only on attributed marketplace orders,
  shown per order.
- Free storefront, order queue, statements, SMS to their customers.
- A photographed catalogue (platform-funded half-day shoot) — a benefit in kind, recorded
  as supply-side CAC.
- Personal onboarding and a named contact; 14-day watch.
- In return: bio link, SLA commitment, verification documents, test order, agreement.

## 3. Pilot demand plan (no paid media until the shelf is ready)

- House audiences via bio links and story mentions (their choice, their voice).
- Founder-produced editorial: "how to read a perfume", house stories (CMS exists, FACT).
- SEO: house and perfume pages server-rendered with metadata (FACT) — the storefronts are
  acquisition surfaces (CLAUDE.md §14).
- Two or three creators with codes, disclosed, performance-paid (Phase 2 mechanics; can be
  manual in the pilot with codes recorded on the order).
- No discount campaigns (brand law; margin).

## 4. What GTM must record from day one

Lead source per house; onboarding hours and shoot cost per house; every campaign or creator
code as an attribution touch; CAC when any spend exists; house-link arrivals vs marketplace
arrivals in the funnel.

## 5. GTM risks (RISK)

Houses join for the free storefront and never route (doc 02 §11 "revise wedge") · founder
time consumed by onboarding · early demand is all seller-originated so the marketplace
stream is never tested · a creator campaign spends before the shelf can convert · name
change after screening (D-1) mid-pilot.

## 6. GTM decisions (OPEN)

wording (counsel) · whether to accept houses outside Cairo/Giza that ship themselves
