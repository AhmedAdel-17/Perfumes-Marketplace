# 3. Attribution rule

Every order must be tagged to a source. Suggested categories: brand
storefront/referral link, organic marketplace search, platform paid
campaign, creator/affiliate, direct repeat, email/CRM and unknown. The
pricing agreement must define attribution windows and dispute handling.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Critical control:** A registered user is not automatically a
  platform-acquired customer. Measure where the order originated and
  whether the customer later returns directly or buys from another brand.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Unit economics equation

Contribution margin per completed order = platform revenue - payment
cost - platform-funded delivery subsidy - refunds/failed-delivery cost -
variable support - promotional subsidy - affiliate/creator cost -
variable fraud loss.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Metric**          **Definition**
  ------------------- ---------------------------------------------------
  AOV                 Average merchandise value of completed orders,
                      excluding shipping unless policy says otherwise

  GMV                 Value of completed merchandise before platform
                      revenue

  Effective take rate Total platform transaction revenue / GMV

  Contribution margin Revenue remaining after all order-variable costs

  CAC                 Acquisition spend / new customers completing first
                      order

  90-day repeat       Customers completing another order within 90 days /
                      eligible first-time customers

  LTV                 Expected cumulative contribution margin per
                      customer; never use revenue as LTV

  Seller payback      Platform acquisition/onboarding cost recovered from
                      seller-linked contribution
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Money flow and payout policy

1.  Customer places order; payment method and seller split are recorded.

2.  Seller confirms inventory within the SLA.

3.  Order is dispatched and tracked to completion/refusal/return.

4.  Platform reconciles merchandise, shipping, discounts, fees and
    adjustments.

5.  Seller payout occurs on a fixed schedule after a defined
    reserve/return period.

6.  Every payout includes an itemized statement and dispute deadline.

The payment structure, ability to hold funds, split settlement and
treatment of COD must be confirmed with a licensed payment provider,
accountant and Egyptian counsel before launch.

# 8. Seller commercial principles

-   No listing fee during the pilot unless a seller requests paid setup
    work.

-   Commission charged only on completed orders; define treatment of
    refused and returned orders.

-   Seller controls retail price subject to truthful display and agreed
    promotion rules.

-   Sponsored placement is labelled and separated from organic review
    quality.

-   Seller receives transaction data necessary to serve its orders;
    platform/customer privacy rules control broader personal data.

-   Platform may suspend unsafe, misleading or operationally harmful
    listings.

# 10. Commercial decision gates

-   Do not subsidize multi-seller shipping until contribution impact is
    measured.

-   Do not open a warehouse because combined shipping sounds attractive;
    use order density and fast-mover data.

-   Do not build an app until repeat direct usage demonstrates that
    installation improves retention or operations.

-   Do not promise seller payouts faster than payment/COD reconciliation
    safely permits.

-   Do not use GMV growth alone; report contribution margin and cash
    conversion.
