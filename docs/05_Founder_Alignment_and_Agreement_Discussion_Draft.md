# 2. Shared intentions

-   Build and validate the venture in good faith, with transparent
    information and documented decisions.

-   Place venture interests and disclosed obligations above hidden side
    arrangements.

-   Do not represent the other founder or bind the venture outside
    agreed authority.

-   Assign venture-created intellectual property to the legal entity
    when formed.

-   Revisit terms through written agreement when facts materially
    change.

documents cannot simply be copied.

# 6. Intellectual property and accounts

-   All code, designs, names, domains, catalogues, research, contracts,
    data models, content and operating materials created for the venture
    are documented and assigned to the company when legally possible.

-   Pre-existing assets are listed explicitly; silence does not create
    an implied licence.

-   Contractors sign confidentiality and IP-assignment terms before work
    begins.

-   Domains, repositories, hosting, payment, analytics and social
    accounts use company-controlled ownership and recovery methods where
    available.

-   No founder may withhold credentials or copy customer/seller data for
    personal use.

# 8. Deadlock procedure

1.  Write the disputed decision, options, evidence, cost and deadline.

2.  Founders meet within three business days and attempt resolution.

3.  If unresolved, consult one mutually agreed adviser or mediator.

4.  If still unresolved, apply the pre-agreed deadlock mechanism drafted
    by counsel; do not improvise a forced transfer after conflict
    begins.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Warning:** A 50/50 split with no deadlock mechanism can paralyse the
  company. Friendship is not a governance system.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Cash, compensation and expenses

-   No salary is owed before a written founder decision or
    employment/service agreement.

-   Approved founder loans are recorded separately from equity and
    reimbursed under written terms.

-   Business expenses require evidence and comply with the approved
    budget.

-   Related-party transactions and founder-owned suppliers require full
    disclosure and both founders\' approval.

-   Bank/payment access requires at least two-person visibility and
    periodic reconciliation.

# 10. Confidentiality, conflicts and competition

Founders protect non-public seller, customer, financial, product and
technical information. Each founder discloses outside work, competing
interests and relationships that could influence decisions. Any
non-compete or non-solicitation restriction must be narrowly drafted and
reviewed for enforceability under applicable law.

                     conduct and immediate controls

                     continuity

  Share transfer     Right of first refusal, permitted transfers,
                     tag/drag and valuation

  Company sale       Approval threshold, proceeds, vesting and founder
                     obligations
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Dispute and governing documentation

Counsel should select Egyptian governing law,
court/arbitration/mediation provisions, language priority, notice method
and interaction with the articles of association and future shareholder
agreement. The signed legal documents must prevail over this discussion
draft.
