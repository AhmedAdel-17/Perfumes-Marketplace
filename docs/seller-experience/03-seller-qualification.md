# Seller qualification framework

## 1. Ideal seller profile (pilot)

Built from doc 01 §4, doc 02 §4 and doc 06 §2–3. Everything in the "ASSUMPTION" column is
to be tested in the 15 seller interviews (doc 02 §10).

| Dimension | Ideal (pilot) | Acceptable | Not now | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Segment | social-first microbrand with branded packaging (S1) | original house (S3); online-first brand (S2) willing to pilot | traditional mixer without documentation (S4); anonymous mixers | doc 02 §4, doc 01 §4 |
| Demand today | real weekly orders through Instagram/WhatsApp | occasional orders, active audience | no audience, no orders | doc 02 §6 ("last real week") |
| Products | 5–20 SKUs, own formulations or clearly labelled inspired products with lawful claims | 3–5 SKUs | designer logos, "identical to X" claims | doc 04 §4, doc 06 §4 |
| Operations | one person answers within a day; ships within two | can commit to SLAs after training | cannot commit to a confirmation SLA | doc 06 §5 |
| Location | Cairo/Giza (D-7) | ships nationally itself | requires platform courier before it exists | doc 01 §7 |
| Documents | owner ID; product responsibility statement; labels | partial, completable in 2 weeks | refuses identity or product documentation | doc 06 §3 |
| Photography | has or will shoot 1000×1250 product photos | accepts paid shoot | refuses | `docs/brand/07` |
| Attitude | wants operations and fair economics; accepts standards | wants demand only | wants a listing with no obligations | doc 01 §4 |
| ASSUMPTION | owner-operated, 1–3 people, 20–200 orders/month | — | — | verify in interviews |

## 2. Qualification scorecard (RECOMMENDATION; thresholds to calibrate after the first ten)

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence (doc 02 §3 rank) |
|---|---|---|
| Live demand (rank 2: routes live orders; rank 4: observed operations) | 30 | last-week order walk-through |
| Product and claims compliance (rank 4) | 25 | photos of labels; claim review |
| Operational readiness (rank 3: signed pilot commitment with date) | 20 | agreed SLA; test order date |
| Brand distinctiveness (rank 5) | 15 | story, packaging, originality |
| Willingness to route orders through DIYAR (rank 3) | 10 | bio link commitment |

Pass: ≥ 70 with no zero in compliance. Anything scored from interview statements alone is
rank 5 evidence and marked as such.

## 3. Onboarding requirements checklist (doc 06 §2–3, as a house sees it)

1. Owner identity and business records as counsel advises (`docs/legal/`).
2. Payout account ownership.
3. Product responsibility: who makes it, batch/label information, warnings.
4. Labels and artwork images.
5. Claims: evidence or removal.
6. Return/defect route: named process and address.
7. Service capacity: one full test order (packaging, confirmation, courier, tracking,
   support).
8. Data/privacy contact.
9. Signed agreement.
10. Catalogue in schema with photographs.
11. Training completed.

## 4. Disqualifiers (hard)

Fake designer logos or bottle copies · unsubstantiated similarity percentages · refusal of
identity/product documentation · unsafe product complaints on record · unwillingness to
accept the SLA and returns policy · reselling other houses' products without their consent
(B-1 open for legitimate multi-brand retailers).

## 5. Pilot cohort shape (RECOMMENDATION)

3–5 houses (doc 02 §9 week 2), mixed: at least one S1 with strong social demand, one S3 for
credibility, one house with an existing website (S2-lite) to test channel conflict. Not more
than five until the payout ledger is live (C1).
