# Seller success and retention model

## 1. What "success" means for a house (measurable)

| Outcome | Metric | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Activated | first completed order within 14 days of go-live | ops |
| Reliable | confirmation on time ≥ 95 %; dispatch on time ≥ 90 %; refusal rate at or below platform median (thresholds ASSUMPTION until pilot data) | house; ops monitors |
| Paid correctly | statements accepted without dispute | finance |
| Growing | attributed marketplace orders/month rising; SKUs published rising | business founder |
| Loyal | keeps the bio link; renews after pilot; refers another house | business founder |

## 2. Success levers (what the platform does)

| Lever | Mechanism | Stage |
|---|---|---|
| Fast first order | ops watches every order for 14 days; personal message on first order | launch |
| Operational clarity | queue by deadline, countdown, coded decisions, pre-breach alerts | operate |
| Money clarity | ledger statement with the deciding attribution touch per order; effective take rate | payout |
| Visibility for performance | merchandising eligibility from indicators (doc 06 §11), never pay-to-rank | grow |
| Editorial support | house story, photographs, category features | launch/grow |
| Fair economics | low fee on own customers, proven | always |

## 3. Retention model

Retention risk signals (leading): declining confirmation speed · stock-outs on top
products · statement disputes · storefront link removed from bio · falling attributed share
(they feel taxed) · no new listings in 60 days.

| Risk | Response |
|---|---|
| Feels taxed | show attribution evidence; review fee terms (D-4 options: minimum fee, subscription) |
| Operationally overwhelmed | staff roles; courier booking; templates for messages |
| Disappointed by demand | honest source mix; do not promise marketplace demand before it exists (doc 02 rule) |
| Channel conflict (S2) | price parity is the house's call; no platform undercutting |
| Trust in payouts | dispute deadline, itemised, ledger visible |

## 4. Seller performance indicators (doc 06 §11, as the house sees them)

Confirmation rate/time · seller-caused cancellation · on-time dispatch · delivery/refusal
pattern (by source) · complaint/defect rate · rating and repeat rate · policy/compliance
events. Shown in the portal with the platform median, never with other houses' names.

## 5. Enforcement ladder (doc 06 §11)

Coaching → written warning and cure plan → listing restriction → temporary suspension →
termination. Each step: indicator, threshold, date, owner, audit entry, seller notification.
Unsafe, fraudulent or severe legal issues skip steps. Thresholds are OPEN until pilot data
(doc 06 §5: "pilot SLA to be measured").

## 6. Seller support standard

One named contact at the platform; response within one business day for operational
questions (ASSUMPTION); statement questions answered with the ledger, not a summary.
