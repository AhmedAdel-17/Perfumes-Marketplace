Ordered by phase (doc 04 §7). Each names the seller value, the platform revenue stream it
relates to (doc 03 §2), prerequisites and the risk of doing it early.

## Growth rules (DOCUMENTED)

- Never sell review ranking (doc 03 §2).
- Do not subsidise multi-seller shipping until measured (doc 03 §10).
- Sponsored placement labelled and separated from organic quality (doc 06 §10).
- Seller receives transaction data necessary to serve its orders; broader personal data
  governed by privacy rules (doc 03 §8).

## Seller referral (RECOMMENDATION, low cost)

A house that refers another verified house receives a documented benefit that is **not** a
rate change (e.g. a paid photo shoot or featured week). Rates stay policy (D-4) and
two-founder approved; benefits in kind do not distort attribution or statements.
