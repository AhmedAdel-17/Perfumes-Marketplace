# 2. Questions the research must answer

1.  Is discovery across local perfume brands painful enough to change
    customer behavior?

2.  Will sellers route existing Instagram/WhatsApp orders through a
    third-party platform?

3.  What value makes sellers accept fees: infrastructure, new demand,
    logistics, payments, analytics or trust?

4.  What is the realistic average order value, gross margin, refusal
    rate and repeat interval?

5.  Which seller segment is easiest to onboard and operationally
    reliable?

6.  What legal/product documentation can serious sellers actually
    provide?

7.  Can the platform create a positive contribution margin before
    central fulfilment?

# 6. Seller discovery interview

Interview the owner or person who manages orders. Ask about the latest
real week, not hypothetical preferences.

-   Walk me through every order received last week, from message to
    payment and delivery.

-   How many inquiries became orders? How many were cancelled or
    refused?

-   Where do customers discover you and where do they complete the
    order?

-   What does one order cost in advertising, packaging, shipping,
    payment and staff time?

-   Which operational step creates the most errors or delay?

-   Have you tried a website or marketplace? What happened?

-   Would you place a third-party storefront link in your bio? Under
    what conditions?

-   What customer and sales data must you retain access to?

-   Would you pay a low fee for seller-generated orders and a higher fee
    for platform-generated orders?

-   What documents, product data and service levels can you provide?

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Do not ask:** Would you use our app? Most people say yes to be
  polite. Ask for evidence: a pilot date, catalogue export, test order or
  bio link.

# 7. Customer interview and survey

## Interview questions

-   Tell me about the last local perfume you bought.

-   Where did you first see it, how did you compare it and why did you
    trust the seller?

-   What information was missing before purchase?

-   Have you had a bad local-perfume or COD delivery experience?

-   When did you last buy from a different brand? Why?

-   Would one checkout across brands help if shipping remained separate?

-   What would make you return to a marketplace rather than buy directly
    next time?

## Survey fields

Collect purchase frequency, last purchase value, brand count considered,
discovery channel, payment preference, location, importance of reviews,
performance information, shipping price sensitivity and willingness to
buy from an unfamiliar verified brand. Separate buyers from non-buyers
in analysis.

# 11. Decision gates

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Decision**       **Evidence**
  ------------------ ----------------------------------------------------
  Proceed to MVP     Multiple sellers route real orders; customers
                     complete purchases; core economics are plausible.

  Revise wedge       Demand exists but sellers resist routing orders, or
                     one seller segment performs much better.

  Operate as seller  Storefront/order tools are valued but customers do
  SaaS first         not cross-shop.

  Pause              Polite interest but no seller commitment, completed
                     orders or contribution path.
