# Product boundaries

## 1. In scope at launch (DOCUMENTED, doc 01 §7 and doc 04 §3)

Responsive website (Arabic; English-ready, D-3 open) · selected local perfume houses ·
seller-managed inventory and fulfilment · structured catalogue, verified reviews, filters ·
COD and licensed e-payment as commercially available · Cairo/Giza operational focus unless
evidence selects another area (D-7) · manual operations where necessary.

## 2. Boundaries by concern (RECOMMENDATION, each traceable)

| Concern | Inside the product | Outside (who owns it) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Category | locally made perfumes, attars, oils, body mists from verified Egyptian houses | imported designer originals; anonymous mixers; unverified claims | doc 01 §4, §7; D-6 open on exact boundaries |
| Supply | vetted houses with the 8 controls | open self-signup | doc 04 §10 |
| Inventory | seller-held | central warehouse (Phase 3) | doc 04 §10 |
| Fulfilment | house books courier (today); platform courier account (Phase 2) | consolidation/combined shipping (Phase 3) | doc 03 §10 |
| Payment | COD; card/wallet via licensed PSP | holding balances, split settlement before D-5 | doc 03 §7 |
| Pricing | seller sets retail price | platform-set prices | doc 03 §8 |
| Promotion | labelled sponsorship; seller promo codes (Phase 2) | selling review ranking | doc 03 §2, doc 06 §10 |
| Claims | moderated; prohibited list enforced | designer-name comparison/search until counsel signs off | doc 04 §4 |
| Support | platform-owned, one route | seller-owned inbox (S-1 open) | doc 01 §6 |
| Identity | phone-first, guest checkout (D-2 open) | social login, accounts required | doc 04 §3, CLAUDE.md §16 |
| Geography | Egypt; Cairo/Giza first | nationwide promise before courier tests; international | doc 01 §7, doc 04 §10 |
| Language | Arabic first | English until D-3 | doc 04 §3 |

## 3. Explicit non-goals (DOCUMENTED, doc 04 §10, doc 01 §7, doc 03 §10)

- Native iOS/Android apps before repeat-usage evidence.
- Central warehouse for the full catalogue.
- Showroom or permanent sample bar.
- Complex AI scent matching before sufficient preference and purchase data.
- Open seller self-approval.
- Unverified user-generated similarity claims promoted by the platform.
- International expansion.
- Platform-held balances without legal/payment review.
- Subsidised multi-seller shipping before contribution impact is measured.
- Complex loyalty points redemption (doc 04 §3 "may follow pilot").

## 5. Rule for changing a boundary

Doc 04 §9 applies: user problem, evidence, success metric, operational owner, legal
dependency, cost — and neither founder changes economics, customer policy or
security-critical architecture alone. A boundary change is recorded in
`docs/company/03-decision-register.md` before it is built.
